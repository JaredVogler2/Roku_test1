# SofaSpark Party Privacy Policy (Draft)

_Last updated November 15, 2026._

SofaSpark Party is an offline, local Roku party game. The app does not require an account and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data with the developer or advertising networks. It has no advertisements, trackers, or custom analytics.

The app stores one small gameplay counter in the Roku device's local application registry. This counter is not transmitted and may be removed when the app is uninstalled or the device is reset.

If a customer purchases the app, Roku handles the store transaction, payment information, and purchase records subject to Roku's own policies. SofaSpark Party does not receive or store payment card details.

For support, contact the address listed in the Roku Streaming Store listing. **Publisher: replace this draft with an approved hosted policy and accurate contact information before publishing.**

This draft describes the code in this repository; the publisher must update it if app features or data practices change.
