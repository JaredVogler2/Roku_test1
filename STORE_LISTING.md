**Full description:**

Turn any living room into game night with SofaSpark Party. Pick a game, choose your crowd, and let the laughs begin. No extra devices or accounts needed — just your Roku remote.

- **ACT IT OUT:** Perform silly scenes in classic charades style.
- **PICK A SIDE:** Debate funny would-you-rather dilemmas.
- **BEAT THE CLOCK:** Name three things in five seconds.
- **STORY SPARK:** Invent wild stories together with fresh plot twists.

Choose Family Night, Friends Night, or Date Night for every game — **12 decks with 432 original prompts**. Handy timers and two-team scoring keep the momentum going. Each deck shuffles its prompts and works offline after the app is installed.

**One price. All games. No ads. No subscriptions.**
